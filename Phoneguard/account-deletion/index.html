PhoneGuard Account & Data Deletion

Manage your PhoneGuard account and delete your data.

Your Data, Your Control

PhoneGuard provides users with options to delete their account and associated data, as well as options to delete specific types of data while keeping their account active.

You can choose between full account deletion and partial data deletion, depending on what you want to remove.

1. Delete Your PhoneGuard Account

If you want to permanently delete your PhoneGuard account, you can initiate the deletion directly from the PhoneGuard app.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the PhoneGuard application.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps shown by the application.
What happens after account deletion?

When your account is deleted, the user account and associated user data stored in the PhoneGuard database will be deleted.

2. Request Account Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have access to the PhoneGuard application, you can request deletion of your PhoneGuard account and associated data by contacting us using the deletion contact below.

Request Account Deletion

Send an account deletion request to:

support@kyvronix.com

Please use the email address associated with your PhoneGuard account where possible. This helps us verify the account and process the request securely.

Request Account Deletion
Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once the deletion has been completed, the account and associated PhoneGuard user data cannot be restored.

3. Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

You do not need to delete your entire PhoneGuard account to remove individual types of data. PhoneGuard provides options to delete certain data while keeping your account.

Intrusion Photos

Users can delete intrusion photos captured and stored by PhoneGuard. Deleting an intrusion photo removes that photo from the user's PhoneGuard data.

Trusted Numbers

Users can remove mobile numbers that they have added to their trusted number list. Removing a trusted number deletes that number from the user's trusted-number data.

Activity Logs

Users can delete their PhoneGuard activity logs. Deleting activity logs removes the selected activity-log data associated with the user's account.

Partial deletion does not delete your account.

Your PhoneGuard account remains active when you delete individual data such as intrusion photos, trusted numbers, or activity logs.

4. Data Deleted During Account Deletion

When a user requests full PhoneGuard account deletion, PhoneGuard deletes the user account and associated user data stored in the PhoneGuard database.

This includes the applicable user data associated with the PhoneGuard account, including data that the user could otherwise delete individually through the application.

  • PhoneGuard user account information
  • Intrusion photos associated with the account
  • Trusted mobile numbers associated with the account
  • Activity logs associated with the account
  • Other user data associated with the account and stored in the PhoneGuard database

5. Deletion Requests

We process account and data deletion requests within a reasonable period after the request is verified.

If additional information is required to verify that a deletion request was submitted by the account owner, we may contact the requester using the provided contact information.

If applicable law requires certain information to be retained, such information may be retained only for the legally required purpose and period. Any such retention will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the PhoneGuard Privacy Policy.

6. Privacy Policy

For more information about how PhoneGuard collects, uses, stores, and protects user data, please review the PhoneGuard Privacy Policy.

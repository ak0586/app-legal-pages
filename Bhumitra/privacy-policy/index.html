This Privacy Policy explains how Kyvronix Technologies ("Data Fiduciary", "we", "us", or "our") processes, stores, protects, and disposes of personal data collected through the BhuMitra: Land Measurement & Area Calculator mobile application and related services.

1. Statutory Roles & Applicability

In adherence to the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023):

  • Data Fiduciary: Kyvronix Technologies, registered in India, determining the purpose and means of processing personal data.
  • Data Principal: You, the natural person using BhuMitra to measure land, calculate plot dimensions, and manage agricultural or real estate records.
  • Data Processors: Infrastructure partners (e.g., Google Firebase, Google Maps Platform, Razorpay) processing data strictly on our instructions under confidentiality agreements.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (Section 4 & 6)

We process your personal and geospatial data solely based on your explicit, informed, and unambiguous consent. When you launch BhuMitra and grant runtime permissions (Location, Storage), you authorize processing strictly for the specified purpose of land measurement and surveying. You may withdraw consent at any time through the in-app settings.

4. Zero Sale of Data & Third-Party Processors

Zero-Monetization Pledge: We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data, GPS survey logs, or land records to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing entities.

We engage only vetted service providers bound by strict confidentiality:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud authentication, database storage, and crash analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform (Google LLC): Map tiles, satellite overlays, and distance/polygon projection. Google Maps Privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Displays banner/interstitial advertisements in the free tier. Users may opt out of personalized ads via device settings or upgrade to the Ad-Free Pro plan.
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Regulated payment gateway for subscription processing. Razorpay Privacy

5. Children's Data Protection (Section 9 DPDP Act)

In strict compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • BhuMitra is designed for adult farmers, landowners, civil engineers, and survey professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors under 18 years of age.
  • We do not conduct behavioral monitoring, profiling, or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a minor has submitted personal details, they may notify our Grievance Officer for immediate erasure.

6. Statutory Rights of the Data Principal (Sections 11–14)

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you are entitled to exercise the following statutory rights:

  1. Right to Access Information (Section 11): Request a summary of your personal data, survey records, and processing activities.
  2. Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Rectify inaccurate survey notes, edit profile information, or request complete, permanent erasure of your account and plot data.
  3. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): File a grievance regarding data privacy with our designated Grievance Officer and receive an acknowledgment within 48 hours.
  4. Right to Nominate (Section 14): Nominate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, shall exercise your rights as a Data Principal.
  5. Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions or withdraw processing consent at any time without penalty.

7. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure

We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active or as required by statutory regulations:

  • Self-Service Erasure: You can delete your account directly inside the app under Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your identity, saved plots, and cloud telemetry.
  • Partial Data Erasure: You can delete individual survey plots under Saved Plots → Delete Plot.
  • Web Erasure Portal: Users who uninstalled the app or lost device access can initiate an erasure request via our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Portal.

    • 8. Technical & Organizational Security Measures (Section 8(5))

    To prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your survey records, we implement industry-standard safeguards:

    • End-to-End Transport Security: All API communication between the app and cloud backend is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
    • Encrypted Database Storage: Cloud Firestore stores records in encrypted format at rest (AES-256).
    • Local Isolation: Plots saved offline remain in sandboxed application storage protected by Android's security sandbox.
